# Listening Study

The listening study will use blinded, randomized pairwise comparisons to test
whether post-trained systems are preferred over registered baselines.

## Planned protocol

- obtain appropriate participant consent before collecting responses
- randomize system order and obscure model identity
- balance samples across songs, singers, and experimental conditions
- include attention and audio-playback checks
- collect dimension-specific judgments in addition to overall preference
- record exclusions and analysis decisions before inspecting final results

The study design, recruitment population, compensation, sample-size rationale,
and statistical analysis will be finalized before data collection. No personal
participant data will be committed to this repository.
