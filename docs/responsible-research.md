# Responsible Research

## Intended use

SingAlign is intended for research on controllable and perceptually aligned
singing voice synthesis using appropriately licensed data and authorized vocal
identities.

## Principal risks

- unauthorized voice imitation or impersonation
- misleading presentation of synthetic performances
- dataset consent, provenance, and licensing failures
- reproduction of demographic or stylistic biases
- copyright concerns involving compositions, lyrics, and recordings
- reward models that encode narrow or harmful aesthetic preferences

## Project safeguards

- review and record dataset terms before use
- do not redistribute restricted datasets or derived artifacts
- disclose synthetic audio in demonstrations
- avoid releases designed for unauthorized identity cloning
- document training provenance, limitations, and evaluation coverage
- assess whether checkpoints or demos require additional release controls

This assessment will be updated whenever the project adds a dataset, model,
human study, public demonstration, or distributable checkpoint.
